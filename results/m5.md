## Readings and checks

- A reported cut is an edit that `find_edits` gives the kind "cut" (positive jump). Its position is `clip_time_s`, the centre of the first frame after the offset change. "Within +-30 s" includes 30 s exactly. The duration error exists only for detected cuts.
- No threshold is applied to the lock of a cut trace. The uncut excerpt goes through the same two steps as a cut trace, and every edit reported on it counts as a false alarm.
- `find_edits` has no frame-weight argument: its fits use the unweighted correlation of `gunj/match.py` on the v2 trace. The search that locates each trace is the weighted one (`gunj/match_v2.py`).
- Checks passed for all 31 streams scored: the trace file and the matcher code have the SHA-256 recorded in the search cache, and the +-1-day search of the uncut trace reproduces the cached search.
- The uncut +-1-day lock is the same reference position as the full-scope lock for 14 of 14 pre-registered streams.
- The cut positions and lengths in the trace files equal `harvest.cut_plan(video_id)` (seeded by the split seed and the video ID) for 31 of 31 streams scored.
- `find_edits` defaults used: {"hop_s": 1.0, "max_edits": 3, "max_shift": 300, "min_gain": 0.15, "min_jump": 5, "min_seg": 180, "offset_s": 8.0, "r_min": 0.6, "relock_radius": 86400, "step": 10, "win": 120}.
