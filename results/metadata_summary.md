## Caveats

- A stream's audio may not come from the venue grid: generators, battery laptops, or remote speakers on video calls. Hum presence is measured later, not assumed.
- Some channels' official status was not verified (see the notes column).
- `release_timestamp` is YouTube's recorded stream start. The 5 Nov probe suggested a constant start latency; the calibration set will measure it.
- 21 kept streams last between 11.8 and 12.05 h, which looks like a YouTube archive length cap. It is not verified here whether such an archive keeps the start or the end of the broadcast, so check its alignment before using `release_timestamp` as truth.
- Channel-search tabs return results by relevance, so those sources are samples of what the search surfaced, not of all streams.
